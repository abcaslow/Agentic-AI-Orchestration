Following the launch of the TypeSafe AI "System One" decision model, Jev,
in September 2026, several major AI players and open-source
projects released competing high-speed, non-generative classification
models: [1, 2]

 - OpenAI – Decisions API: Launched on October 6, 2026, this is OpenAI's
 direct commercial answer to Jev, designed specifically for rapid data
 classification and automated decision-making workflows. [1]
 - Amazon Web Services – Strands Decider 2B: An open-source alternative
 built by Amazon to mimic Jev's fast, low-cost categorization capabilities,
 designed to run locally on a smaller footprint. [2]
 - Laya: A prominent open-source community alternative that runs locally
 and offers ultra-low latency response times (around 30ms) for
 machine-actionable judgments. [3]
