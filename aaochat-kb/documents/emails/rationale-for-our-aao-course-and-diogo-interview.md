Nov. 2022: The Conversational AI Era Begins. Have a Chat with CHAT-GPT

Over the last four years, the world has seen the incredible potential of
Artificial Intelligence. It all started with the release of OpenAI’s CHAT-GPT
3.5 on November 30.2022.
<https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/november-30/chatgpt-released-openai>
From this date and up until May, 2025, the entire world was amazed by what
appeared to be the next generation of Google Search. Through a web
interface, CHAT-GPT users would type in questions and CHAT-GPT would supply
answers with detailed explanations. Furthermore, users would “prompt
CHAT-GPT” to write a letter, a book report, computer code, perform legal
research and CHAT-GPT would actually write the letter,book report,
computer code and provide responses to a legal research request and so much
more. Many times CHAT-GPT would “hallucinate” and make mistakes and some
called AI the “greatest bullshit generator” ever invented. But as there is
a famous saying in AI and data science, “all models are wrong but some are
useful” and while AI still can be wrong, it is still useful. Conclusion: AI
is here and millions of people are using it. Whether you love, hate or are
skeptical about AI and you do not trust it, it is not going away,.It is
highly recommended that you learn as much about AI as possible. Being
ignorant about AI has no benefit.

Agentic AI as Introduced by Anthropic’s Claude Code Outshines CHAT-GPT
