Jev (from TypeSafe AI, not TypeScript) is named after economist *William
Stanley Jevons
<https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F01dc_9&q=William+Stanley+Jevons>*
. [1 <https://www.firecrawl.dev/blog/what-is-jev>]

 - *Jevons' Paradox:* The name nods to Jevons' economic paradox, which
 states that as the efficiency of using a resource increases, the total
 consumption of that resource tends to increase rather than decrease. [1
 <https://www.firecrawl.dev/blog/what-is-jev>]
 - *Application to AI:* TypeSafe AI applies this concept to AI inference,
 betting that making a decision cheap and fast enough means people will make
 vastly more decisions. [1 <https://www.firecrawl.dev/blog/what-is-jev>]

large language models (LLMs) or how its *System One* architecture works.
