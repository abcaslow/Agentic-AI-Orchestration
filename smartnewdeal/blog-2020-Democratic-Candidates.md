**Author:** Bernie Wolzak | August 9, 2019 | Category: 2020 Presidential Election
**Captured:** 2026-07-08

Based on a Quinnipiac University poll (via Real Clear Politics), the top five front runners for the 2020 Democratic Nomination were: Joe Biden (31.0%), Bernie Sanders (15.8%), Elizabeth Warren (15.5%), Kamala Harris (8.3%), Pete Buttigieg (5.5%).

Tier Two candidates: Beto O'Rourke (2.5%), Cory Booker (2.3%), Andrew Yang (1.5%), Tulsi Gabbard (1.3%). The remaining candidates are classified as the "Expendables."

From a fundraising perspective (Q2 2019), the ordering shifts: **Pete Buttigieg is the #1 fundraiser.** Both Buttigieg and Biden raised twice as much as #5 fundraiser Harris. Tier 2 fundraisers: Booker ($4.5M), Klobuchar ($3.9M), Inslee ($3.8M), O'Rourke ($3.6M), Yang and Castro ($2.8M each).

The author profiles the three top-tier candidates, noting all three are "O-L-D":
1. **Joe Biden** (76) — the Obama Legacy candidate; US Senator from Delaware for 36 years; VP 2009–2017; pro-ACA, not for Medicare for All; favors enforcing immigration laws.
2. **Bernie Sanders** (77) — an uncompromising Democratic Socialist; Medicare for All, Free College, immediate climate action; against the Trans-Pacific Partnership.
3. **Elizabeth Warren** (70) — supports many Sanders policies but identifies as a "capitalist." The author predicts Warren will out-maneuver Biden and Bernie (à la Bill Clinton vs. Paul Tsongas, 1992), positioning herself as a "progressive capitalist." The BIG QUESTION: can she beat Trump?

Part II is promised, focusing on the ideas of Tier 2 and Tier 3 candidates that risk being muted if the race narrows to "the battle of the 70 year olds" — especially Andrew Yang's emphasis on cloud services, 5G, and AI. "Their ideas are N-O-T expendable!"
