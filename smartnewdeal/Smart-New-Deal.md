# Smart New Deal — United with Code

**Source:** https://smartnewdeal.com/smart-new-deal/
**Captured:** 2026-07-08

The Smart New Deal advocates improving the effectiveness of STEM education while dramatically reducing its cost by exploring the blending of traditional education methods with a cloud based education infrastructure. The current education ecosystem that has organically evolved on the Internet over the past 20 years provides many of the key building blocks to construct a cloud based education infrastructure.

From a technical perspective, the primary contribution of the Smart New Deal to building a cloud based education infrastructure is outlined in **US Patent #11669350** titled *Virtual Desktop Multiplexing for Video and Audio Conferencing* (https://patents.google.com/patent/US11669350B1/en). Using the technology outlined in this patent, millions of students studying any STEM subject that involves a computer interface can be assigned their own virtual desktop in the cloud which in turn can be inserted into a grid of virtual desktops to dramatically improve on-line education and collaboration at a dramatically lower cost.

It is the hope of the Smart New Deal that a new generation of American leadership at the federal, state and local levels will explore the ideas presented in the accompanying 40-page PDF (https://smartnewdeal.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Smart-New-Deal-v9.9.pdf).
