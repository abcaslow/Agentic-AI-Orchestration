# The UnitedwithCode Story (About Us) — United with Code

**Source:** https://smartnewdeal.com/about-us/
**Captured:** 2026-07-08

**Developing Training Programs for the Next Generation in a World Dominated by Software**

> "Software is Eating the World. We Must Develop a Higher Level of Universal Software Literacy"

Founded in 2017, UnitedwithCode, LLC is a minority owned, woman owned IT training company based in the Washington DC Metro area. It is a family run business operated by the Caslow Trio: (1) **Lania Caslow**, born in Guatemala and raised in Washington DC; (2) **Bruce Caslow**, a native of Baltimore, Maryland, a published author and highly experienced technical trainer with over 30 years experience; and (3) their son **Andrew Caslow**, a young entrepreneur and student at the University of California at Berkeley majoring in Data Science.

## Pre-COVID Focus: Python-centric IT Automation Training
Up until COVID-19, UnitedwithCode focused on delivering Python-centric IT automation training for network engineers transitioning to software defined networking (SDN) and Infrastructure as Code, driven by Bruce Caslow's experience as an international Cisco CCIE instructor and content developer. In 1999, Bruce published the first textbook to prepare students for the Cisco CCIE lab: *Cisco Certification, Bridges, Routers and Switches for CCIE's* (Prentice Hall, 840 pages). With Val Pavlichenko, Bruce co-founded **NetMasterClass** (2001), which developed content and assessment software for the Cisco 360 CCIE Learning Program (purchased and launched by Cisco Systems in 2008). In February 2017, Bruce taught the first Cisco Network Programmability course (NPDESI) to Cisco TAC engineers in RTP, North Carolina, followed by a class at Cisco World Headquarters in San Jose in March 2017.

## 2019: Promoting Universal Literacy in Automation, Data Science, ML & AI
As the 2020 campaign began, the Caslow Trio was concerned no candidate except Andrew Yang was talking about how software and AI impact Americans' lives. Lania Caslow, a Hispanic woman and product of the DC Public School system, wanted to help under-represented segments prepare for software-centric technologies and deliver 21st-century course content to DC-area youth. Their son Andrew (then 16) began studying Python and reading about Data Science, ML and AI. He entered C-SPAN StudentCam 2020 and won an award for his video *America in the Age of Artificial Intelligence*, and met Andrew Yang at a rally in Manchester, New Hampshire.

## Post-COVID Focus: IT Automation Training and Data Science Training
During lockdown, Andrew (then 17) further developed his Python skills and discovered UC Berkeley's **Unboxing Data Science (BUDS)** program for high schoolers, using its free Python-centric Data Science content. Returning to Episcopal High School (Alexandria, VA) for senior year, he worked on a smartphone-based COVID contact-tracing project and was quoted in the Washington Post and Bloomberg News. Family friend and world-class software developer **Brent Baccala of Freesoft** developed the **Collaborate** distance-learning platform (patent pending) that lets an instructor see a grid of student virtual desktops in parallel and in real time.

## Spring 2021: Data Science for High School (DS4HS) Launched
For his Episcopal High School externship (the Washington Program), Andrew proposed **Data Science for High School (DS4HS)** to Mr. Louis Smith (Director of Equity and Community), emphasizing reaching a diverse set of students. Using UC Berkeley BUDS content and Brent Baccala's Collaborate system, Andrew launched DS4HS with students from seven DC-area high schools plus one from Jacksonville, Florida. Over summer 2021, DS4HS courseware was revised and Andrew recorded several hours of Python for Data Science modules (Jupyter Notebooks) freely available on YouTube. In Fall 2021, UnitedwithCode supported a Northern Virginia group, Today's Students Tomorrow's Leaders (TS2L). Andrew is now a freshman at UC Berkeley (rated by Forbes as a top US university; Berkeley's Data Science program and the Data8 intro course, http://data8.org/, are widely used and freely available).

## Commitment to Give Back
Both Bruce (Baltimore City Public Schools; Baltimore Polytechnic Institute "Poly") and Lania (DC Public Schools) are products of public schools. In 2005, Bruce returned to Poly to teach a pro bono computer networking class using NetMasterClass software. UnitedwithCode's two programs — (1) Python-centric IT automation training for network engineers and (2) the DS4HS program founded by Andrew — are committed to empowering people through hands-on technical education. Given Lania's Guatemalan roots, it is her dream to deliver DS4HS in both English and Spanish across the US and Latin America.
