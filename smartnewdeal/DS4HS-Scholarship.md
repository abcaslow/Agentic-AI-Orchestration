# Data Science for High School Scholarship Program — United with Code

**Source:** https://smartnewdeal.com/scholarships/
**Captured:** 2026-07-08

UnitedwithCode is committed to giving back to the community with Data Science for High School Scholarships. Using a suite of state of the art Internet technologies — cloud services, e-learning systems and more — UnitedwithCode wants to demonstrate to the world that a quality on-line education for everyone is a click away. All students need is a computer, a web browser and an Internet connection.

The UnitedwithCode Scholarship program is designed to leverage this technology for any student that has an interest in the field of Data Science. The scholarship program will bring students together from around the nation and even around the world into a grid of virtual-desktops where they can be taught and can socialize with other students. In every neighborhood in the world, talent can be found. UnitedwithCode wants to find this distributed talent and bring it all together in its Data Science for High School program.

Each scholarship recipient will receive their own Virtual-Desktop and will be enrolled in UnitedwithCode's **Data Science for High School** Course.

Contact UnitedwithCode at training@unitedwithcode.com if you want to help support and promote the Data Science for High School Scholarship Program, or to sponsor or recommend a scholar.
