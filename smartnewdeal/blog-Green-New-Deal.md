# Dad, I read the Green New Deal (H.RES.-109/116th Congress); It's an easy read. — United with Code (Blog)

**Source:** https://smartnewdeal.com/dad-i-read-the-green-new-deal-its-an-easy-read/
**Author:** Andres Wolzak | August 10, 2019 | Category: Ideology
**Captured:** 2026-07-08

The author read the Green New Deal (H.RES.-109). It's only 14 pages — and because of the Congressional format, actually about 6 pages of regularly formatted text. A really easy read. Direct link: https://www.congress.gov/116/bills/hres109/BILLS-116hres109ih.pdf

A fun exercise: open the PDF and press CTRL+F to count how often specific words/phrases appear, e.g. "New Deal" or "socialism." Answer: "socialism" appears **ZERO** times. Given that, many of the Green New Deal's objectives could be achieved via (1) socialist, (2) capitalist, or (3) hybrid methods.

Structure: the first four pages are seven "whereas" statements; the remaining 10 pages outline four resolution sections (Section 1 with 5 sub-sections, Section 2 with 14, Section 3 with 1, Section 4 with 15). The first two "whereas" clauses present facts about climate change and its costs; the third shifts into economic and social injustices (which, per the author, disrupted the flow and lost focus on climate change). On page 4, the last two "whereas" clauses reference FDR's New Deal and World War II, framing a "historic opportunity" to launch the Green New Deal with similar energy.

The document distinguishes (1) Green New Deal Goals and (2) Green New Deal Mobilization.

**Concluding comments:** The resolution is 100% aspirational — it sets objectives and goals but no specific plans, so it can be attained via socialist, capitalist, or combined methods. While Bernie Sanders and AOC may be "Democratic Socialists," Elizabeth Warren, Pete Buttigieg, Andrew Yang, John Delaney, and Michael Bennett are capitalists who want to adjust America's capitalist environment to attain many of the same goals. The author encourages using congress.gov research tools (search, alerts) and exploring the resolution's Actions, Co-sponsors, Committees, and Related Bills tabs.
