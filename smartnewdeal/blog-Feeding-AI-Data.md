# Feeding AI Data — United with Code (Blog)

**Source:** https://smartnewdeal.com/feeding-ai-data/
**Author:** Bernie Wolzak | August 20, 2019 | Category: Technology
**Captured:** 2026-07-08

AI systems function best with clearly defined win/loss outcomes. Examples of clearly defined win/loss outcomes are: (1) playing a game like chess or checkers — you either win or lose; (2) investing — you either make money or lose money; or (3) analyzing x-rays — you either find the fractured bone or you don't.

For each of these applications, you can feed the specific AI "Expert System" massive amounts of data:

1. **Chess AI:** Have the system play itself one million times using thousands of opening moves used by chess masters. With each game it gets better — "it's all about the reps." Google's AlphaZero does not learn chess from humans; it learns to play chess from itself.
2. **Finance/investing AI:** Feed it volumes of historical financial data and validate against actual market/company performance.
3. **Financial-crime detection AI:** Look for abnormalities in financial institutions to detect financial crimes or reckless risk — the kind of AI designed to avoid massive bank failures like the 2008–2009 banking crisis.
4. **X-ray AI:** Feed it thousands of historical x-rays with a pre-determined correct analysis and measure its ability to derive the correct analysis. The system learns from failure and improves with each unique x-ray.

You can cross-reference AI systems — have one AI system evaluate another. Finally, in AI development: (1) false positives are actually not a problem, but (2) false negatives ARE a problem.
